MyDSM's blurbs

About me:

I'm a messenger from the good old days, rebuilt for today. Remember coming home from school, signing in, and hearing that little chime when your friends came online? Changing your personal message to a song lyric? Spending an entire evening making your page look just right? That's me.

I live on your desktop, every chat gets its own window, and closing me just tucks me into the tray so you never miss a message.

Who I'd like to meet:

Anyone who misses buddy lists, glossy buttons and emoticons with personality. Your friends, your cousins, that group from summer camp. Bring everyone.

MyDSM's privacy (the honest version)
  • People find you by your @username. Your e-mail is never needed and never shown.
  • Messages and files are encrypted on the way and stored encrypted on the server.
  • They are not end-to-end encrypted: the server can read them, so moderators can act on reports and keep MyDSM safe. Opening a conversation requires a written reason and is always logged.
  • Your profile page is visible to your contacts only, unless you choose to open it to everyone.
  • This website has no trackers, no cookies and no ads.
  • Read the full Rules and Privacy Policy. Everyone accepts them when they sign up.

jumpy_jo
Why does my phone say MyDSM may be harmful?
Google Play Protect checks every app installed from outside the Play Store. MyDSM is new, few people have installed it yet, and it asks for things a chat app needs but that Google's automatic check treats with caution: it stays connected in the background so your messages arrive (MyDSM uses no outside push service, only our own server), it asks to skip battery saving for the same reason, and it can install its own updates so you never have to download it by hand again. Nothing in MyDSM is harmful: it is signed with our own key, and Android only installs updates signed with that same key. Tap Install anyway. We are asking Google to review MyDSM so the warning goes away.
